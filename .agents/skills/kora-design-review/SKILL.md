# Kigae Design Review

Use this skill when the task concerns the Kigae public website, dynamic template
catalog, module or integration pages, onboarding, or cross-sell and upgrade
flows. It complements the general `impeccable` and `design-taste-frontend`
skills; those remain the source for implementation craft and visual direction.

## Required context

Read [the Kigae website review field guide](../../../docs/design/kora-website-review-field-guide.md),
the [product website and onboarding research library](../../../docs/design/kora-product-website-onboarding-research-library.md),
and the marketplace plan at
`/home/asena/projects/kora-cloud/docs/template-marketplace-implementation-plan.md`.
Inspect the target surface and its actual responsive and interactive behavior
before recommending changes.

The field guide contains the 20-video YC design reference set and transcript
summaries for the three videos supplied by the user. The research library
contains books, NN/g and GOV.UK research sources, and a research-to-design
workflow for website discovery and onboarding. Use these as starting points;
do not imply all videos have been transcribed or that general guidance has
already been validated with Kigae customers.

## Review focus

1. Check that a first-time visitor can understand the audience, business
   outcome, product, proof, and next step.
2. Check that template and module pages show their relationships in plain
   language, with required and optional choices, dependencies, workflow maps,
   compatibility, and plan impact.
3. Check that onboarding explains what will be installed and provides visible
   feedback for selection, validation, loading, completion, and errors.
4. Check mobile, keyboard, reduced-motion, and slow-network behavior. Do not
   rely on hover or animation to reveal essential content.
5. Treat AI-generated copy, art, and motion as drafts; retain them only when
   they clarify the offer or express Kigae's own identity.
6. Report concrete findings with evidence, user impact, and a recommended fix.
   Prioritize comprehension, trust, accessibility, and activation over polish.

## Source-backed guidance

- For discovery, journey mapping, onboarding, and validation, follow the
  research workflow and source distinctions in the [Kigae research
  library](../../../docs/design/kora-product-website-onboarding-research-library.md).
- Treat the current discovery-to-first-task sequence as a hypothesis. Find out
  which entry routes, decisions, prerequisites, and first outcomes matter for
  Kigae's target customers before making it the only path.

- Use the website narrative and progressive-disclosure ideas from [How Stripe
  Built Their New Website](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ypzNhwpmOD4).
- Use the AI output review and purposeful interaction ideas from [Common
  Mistakes With Vibe-Coded
  Websites](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DNSXlBmukck).
- Use visible signifiers, hierarchy, grouping, and feedback patterns from
  [Every UI/UX Concept Explained in Under 10 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EcbgbKtOELY).

The videos provide heuristics, not requirements. Verify decisions with Kigae
customers and product evidence.
